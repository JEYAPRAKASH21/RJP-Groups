Available Services & Document Checklist

Explore government identity cards, revenue certificates, bill payments, and banking services with full document requirement checklists.

🪪

Aadhaar Card Services

Aadhaar enrollment, mobile number linking, biometric update, address correction & color PVC card printing.

Required Documents Checklist
  • Original Proof of Identity (Voter ID / Driving License)
  • Proof of Address (Ration Card / EB Bill)
  • Active Mobile Number for OTP Verification
⏱️ Processing: Same Day / 48 hrs
💳

PAN Card (New & Correction)

Instant e-PAN generation, new NSDL/UTI PAN card applications, and name/DOB/photo corrections.

Required Documents Checklist
  • Aadhaar Card copy (Name & DOB matching)
  • 2 Passport Size Photographs
  • Existing PAN Card copy (For corrections)
⏱️ Instant e-PAN / 7 Days Physical
📜

Income & Community Certificates

Official Revenue e-Sevai certificates for Income, Community, Nativity, OBC & First Graduate status.

Required Documents Checklist
  • Smart Ration Card & Applicant Aadhaar Card
  • Applicant Passport Size Photograph
  • Self-Declaration Form & Parent Certificate copy
⏱️ Processing: 3–5 Business Days
🛂

Passport & Voter ID Services

Fresh Passport application, appointment slot booking, renewal & Voter ID card registration/address change.

Required Documents Checklist
  • Aadhaar Card, PAN Card & Bank Passbook
  • 10th Marksheet or Birth Certificate
  • Current Residence Proof & Passport Photos
⏱️ Slot Booking: Instant
🏦

AEPS Banking & Money Transfer

Aadhaar Enabled Payment System (AEPS) biometric cash withdrawal, mini statements & instant DMT money transfers.

Required Documents Checklist
  • Aadhaar Card Number for Biometric Auth
  • Beneficiary Bank Account Number & IFSC Code
  • Active Mobile Number for Transaction Receipt
⏱️ Transaction: Instant (0 Sec)
⚡

EB Bills, FASTag & Insurance

TNEB electricity bill payments, LIC premium payments, instant vehicle FASTag recharge & motor insurance renewals.

Required Documents Checklist
  • TNEB Consumer Number / EB Receipt
  • Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC Book)
  • LIC Policy Number / Existing Policy Copy
⏱️ Payment Receipt: Instant
